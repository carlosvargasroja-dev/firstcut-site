Privacy policy

This policy explains what FirstCut does with information it receives when its owner connects a social account such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X or Twitch.

Who uses FirstCut

Only the owner of the computer it runs on. FirstCut does not offer accounts to anyone else and does not collect information about other people.

What FirstCut receives

  • Basic profile details of the owner’s own connected account: an account id, display name and profile picture.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let FirstCut act for that account within the permissions the owner approved.
  • View, like and comment counts for videos the owner posted, so the owner can see how each clip performs.
  • On Twitch, the chat messages of a stream the owner is watching, read only to measure how busy the chat is. Messages are counted, not stored.

TikTok permissions

PermissionWhy FirstCut asks for it
user.info.basicShow which TikTok account is connected.
video.upload, video.publishPost a clip to the owner’s TikTok account when the owner presses Post.
video.list, user.info.statsRead the view counts of the owner’s own posted videos.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner’s computer, in a local database and local files. FirstCut has no server of its own and sends this information nowhere except back to the platform it came from, through that platform’s API.

What FirstCut never does

  • It never sells, rents or shares information with anyone.
  • It never uses information for advertising or profiling.
  • It never posts without the owner pressing Post, and on TikTok every post is started by the owner.

Removing access and data

The owner can disconnect an account on FirstCut’s Setup screen, which deletes its tokens from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time from the platform’s own settings; on TikTok that is Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services. Deleting FirstCut’s data folder removes everything it stored.

Children

FirstCut is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
